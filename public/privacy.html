Privacy Policy

Flipping Husks · Last updated: June 17, 2026

This policy explains what information Flipping Husks ("the Game", "we", "us") processes and why. The Game is a free multiplayer card game, playable in a web browser and as a Discord Activity. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Discord Inc.

Information we process

When you play as a Discord Activity, with your authorization Discord shares a limited profile with us through the identify scope:

  • your Discord user ID,
  • your Discord username / display name, and
  • your avatar.

We also receive the voice channel identifier of the channel you launched the Activity in. This is used only to place everyone in that channel into the same game room.

When you play on the website, we process only the display name you type and the room code you enter.

Gameplay data — the cards, scores, and room state of an active match — is generated as you play.

How we use it

  • To show your name (and avatar, in Discord) to the other players in your game.
  • To group players from the same voice channel into one room.
  • To run the game logic (turns, scores, rounds) during a match.

Storage and retention

We do not use a database and we do not create lasting accounts. All game and player information is held only in the server's temporary memory for the duration of a session. It is automatically discarded when the game ends or the room becomes inactive, and it does not survive a server restart. We do not retain your information after your session.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their own purposes. Your name and avatar are visible only to other players in your own game. The Game is hosted on Render, and Discord provides the Activity platform; both process traffic solely to deliver the Game to you.

Your choices

You control what Discord shares by approving or declining the authorization prompt, and you can stop sharing at any time by leaving the Activity. Because nothing is stored beyond your session, there is no stored profile to delete.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly process their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
